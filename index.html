Research

Published work at the intersection of robot learning, simulation, and dexterous manipulation.

IEEE 2025

A Pathway Study for Future Humanoid Standards

Kartik Sachdev et al. (70+ robotics experts from industry, academia & regulatory bodies)

IEEE Robotics & Automation Society

A strategic roadmap developed by over 70 experts identifying critical gaps in existing robotics standards for humanoid robots. Addresses three interconnected pillars — Classification, Stability, and Human-Robot Interaction — proposing a coordinated path to certify the safety of human-robot interactions. Current standards designed for fixed industrial arms are insufficient for the unique challenges posed by humanoids.

ResearchGate PDF
CoRL 2025

Sim-to-Real Reinforcement Learning for Vision-Based Dexterous Manipulation on Humanoids

Toru Lin, Kartik Sachdev, Linxi Fan, Jitendra Malik, Yuke Zhu

UC Berkeley · NVIDIA · UT Austin

A practical sim-to-real RL recipe for training humanoid robots on challenging dexterous tasks — grasp-and-reach, box lift, and bimanual handover. Features automated real-to-sim tuning, generalized contact-and-goal rewards, divide-and-conquer policy distillation, and a hybrid object representation with modality-specific augmentation. Achieves high success rates on unseen objects.

arXiv PMLR Project Page
NeurIPS Workshop 2023

Learning Dark Matter Representation from Strong Lensing Images through Self-Supervision

Yashwardhan Deshmukh, Kartik Sachdev, Michael Toomey, Sergei Gleyzer

NeurIPS 2023 Workshop: Machine Learning and the Physical Sciences · ML4Sci / GSoC

Self-supervised learning applied to gravitational lensing images to develop unsupervised embeddings across three dark matter models — ultra-light axions, cold dark matter, and halos without substructure. Implements contrastive learning, BYOL, SimSiam, and DINO using ResNet50 and Vision Transformer architectures, demonstrating that self-supervised methods consistently outperform supervised counterparts on classification and regression tasks.
